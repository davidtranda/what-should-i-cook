# 🍳 What Should I Cook?

> A smart cooking recommendation app that helps users decide what to cook based on the ingredients they already have.

---

## 📖 About

One of the most common daily questions is:

> **"What should I cook today?"**

While there are thousands of recipe websites and apps, they all expect the user to search through hundreds of recipes.

**What Should I Cook** takes a different approach.

Instead of asking:

> "Which recipe do you want?"

it asks:

> "What do you already have?"

Based on the ingredients available, the user's cooking history and the products that are about to expire, the application recommends a few meals that best fit the current situation.

The goal isn't to teach users how to cook.

The goal is to help them decide **what** to cook.

---

## ✨ Main Features

- 🥚 Kitchen inventory management
- 🛒 Smart shopping list
- 🍽️ Personalized meal recommendations
- ⏳ Time-based suggestions
- 📅 Cooking history
- ❤️ Favorite meals
- 📦 Automatic inventory updates after cooking
- ♻️ Food waste reduction
